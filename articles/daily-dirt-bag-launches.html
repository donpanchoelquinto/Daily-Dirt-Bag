Daily Dirt Bag Launches; Collin "Not Surprised," Mildly Flattered

A bold new publication promises daily coverage of the stories that matter, to us.

By Marguerite "Midge" Fontaine, Senior Dirt Correspondent · October 12, 2022 · 2 min read

A new voice entered the media landscape on Wednesday as the Daily Dirt Bag officially opened its doors, its inbox, and, regrettably, its comment section. The publication describes itself as "a crude attempt at being crude," a mission statement that editors say they have already exceeded.

"We looked at the news industry and asked ourselves what was missing," said Senior Dirt Correspondent Marguerite "Midge" Fontaine. "The answer was a lot of things, but we picked the one that was funny."

The first issue's lead story, a four-paragraph investigation into who ate the last slice of something at a gathering, took six weeks to report and was, by the staff's own admission, "inconclusive but very fun." A follow-up is planned. A suspect has been named. The suspect has denied everything, with crumbs on his shirt.

Our editorial standards

  • We will report only the truth, as long as the truth is funny.
  • We will correct our errors, but only the ones that are not funnier left alone.
  • We will never, under any circumstances, apologize to Collin before breakfast.
  • All opinions expressed in the comments belong to the commenters, who we assume are lovely people.

Collin, the subject of the publication's launch edition, issued a short statement calling the site "a lot of effort for something so obviously unnecessary." Sources say he bookmarked it within the hour and has visited it several times since, a detail he has declined to confirm or deny.

He said it was unnecessary. Then he asked where he could subscribe. That is called a review.

Midge Fontaine, Senior Dirt Correspondent

Early reader feedback has been warm. A letter from a reader in a neighboring county praised the paper for "saying what everyone was thinking, and for being sort of mean about it in a way that's still polite." The letter was unsigned and appeared to be typed on the same machine as the Department of Common Decency's.

The Daily Dirt Bag intends to publish daily, or at least frequently, or whenever someone has something to say. Subscribers will be notified by email, and Collin will be notified by his friends, in person, at inconvenient times.
