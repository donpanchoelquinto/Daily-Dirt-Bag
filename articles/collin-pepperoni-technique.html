Collin Invents a New Method for Cutting Pepperoni, Then Eats It Whole

He saws. The pepperoni does not notice. He eats it anyway.

By Marjorie Ng, Food Desk · November 26, 2026 · 2 min read

Collin has spent the past several weeks learning how to cut pepperoni properly. He has not learned how to cut pepperoni. He has, however, invented a method, and he has demonstrated it at a dinner gathering with the calm of a man who has never once been told he is doing it wrong.

The method works like this. First, he pins a single slice of pepperoni to the plate with the tines of his fork. Second, he lays the blade of a dinner knife flat across the top of the slice. Third, he saws. He saws quickly, back and forth, for several seconds, with real conviction, as if the pepperoni had said something about his mother.

The knife is working. The pepperoni is not. Collin has decided these are two separate facts that do not need to be discussed at the table.

Marjorie Ng, Food Desk

Results, by stage

  • Sawing. Vigorous. Visible from across the room. Did not cut anything.
  • Lifting. He raised the fork, checked the slice, and found it intact, as it had been before he began.
  • Eating. He ate the whole slice in one bite, which he described, without speaking, as "the cut."
  • Repeat. He moved to a second slice, sawed it with the same confidence, and ate that one whole too.

Guests at the table watched in silence, the kind usually reserved for magic tricks and car accidents. Nobody asked him to stop. One guest said the method was "technically a cut, if you believe in cuts," and asked for the salad.

Collin has not commented on his method's efficiency, nor on the amount of pepperoni consumed. He says he is still refining the technique and hopes to introduce a second step, which he has not yet decided on, and which he says will involve "more knife."

At press time the pepperoni was reported to be fine. It had not been cut, but it had certainly been through something, and Collin's expression, throughout, remained the expression of a man who knows exactly what he is doing.
