Collin Confidently Gives Directions; Everyone Else Arrives First

"Just take a left at the big tree," he said. Nobody has located the tree.

By Tobias Wren, Travel & Navigation Desk · March 19, 2024 · 2 min read

A routine group dinner turned into a regional navigation incident on Tuesday afternoon when Collin, speaking with great confidence and no map, offered directions to a restaurant he had visited once, in the dark, as a passenger.

"Just take a left at the big tree," Collin told the group by phone. When pressed on which tree, he clarified that it was "the big one." Witnesses report that the area contained, at minimum, several hundred trees, of which a respectable number were big.

The tree in question has since become a local landmark of sorts. Three independent teams have surveyed the area. One reported a "large oak," one a "medium pine," and one simply wrote "there are a lot of trees, why would you say that," before leaving the project.

How the afternoon unfolded

  • 3:40 p.m. Collin departs. Declines the offered GPS on the grounds that he "basically lives here."
  • 4:15 p.m. A search party is dispatched after Collin texts the single word "hm."
  • 4:32 p.m. The rest of the group, who ignored the directions entirely, sit down and order appetizers.
  • 5:10 p.m. Collin arrives, claims he "took the scenic route," and asks if there is any bread left.

Travel & Navigation Desk correspondent Tobias Wren, who has never been lost, reviewed the route after the fact and described it as "less a path and more a series of confident decisions."

In forty years of covering navigation, I have never seen a man get lost with so much certainty.

Tobias Wren, Travel & Navigation Desk

The group, for its part, took the incident in stride. "We've learned to open a map the moment he says 'I know a shortcut,'" said one attendee. "It's less about trust and more about survival." Another added that the appetizers, in fairness, were excellent, and that the delay had really let them "settle in."

Asked whether he would reconsider giving directions in the future, Collin replied that the instructions were "basically right" and that the restaurant had "moved." The restaurant has not moved. We checked.
