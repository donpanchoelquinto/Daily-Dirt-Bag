Collin Arrives "Right on Time" to Event That Ended Forty Minutes Ago

He brought a folding chair. Nobody has asked why.

By Rafael Quintero, Local Events Reporter · June 22, 2021 · 2 min read

Collin walked into a community gathering on Monday evening carrying a folding chair, a bag of chips, and the unshakeable belief that he was exactly on time. The event, which had concluded forty minutes earlier, was by then a room of stacked tables and a single, tired man with a broom.

"He came in smiling," said Local Events Reporter Rafael Quintero, who had been there since 4:30 p.m. and was therefore, as always, early. "He looked around, nodded at the empty chairs, and said, 'Looks like I beat the rush.'"

The evening, by all accounts, had been a success. More than sixty people attended, a local band played three songs and a fourth "by request," and a raffle prize of a gently used slow cooker went to a delighted winner who is, Quintero noted, "still telling the story." Collin missed all of it, and the slow cooker.

Organizers confirmed the final raffle had already been drawn, the potluck had been packed into reusable containers, and the keynote speaker had been driven home. Collin was offered a leftover roll, which he accepted, and a tour of the empty room, which he also accepted.

He walked the whole space like a man inspecting a property he was about to buy. Then he thanked us for 'having him.'

Rafael Quintero, Local Events Reporter

When asked how he had arrived so late with so much confidence, Collin said the invitation had "said six-ish," and that, in his experience, "ish" was a generous window. The invitation said four o'clock. There was no ish on it.

The event's volunteer coordinator offered Collin a ride home, which he declined, insisting he'd "just wait a few minutes in case anyone else shows up." She reported that the parking lot was, at that point, entirely empty, and that his chair was facing the door "with great hope."

At last report Collin had set up his folding chair in the parking lot, "just in case," and was facing the building.
