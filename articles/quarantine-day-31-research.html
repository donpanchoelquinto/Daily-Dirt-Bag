Quarantine Day 31: Collin Has Rewatched the Same Show Four Times and Calls It "Research"

Asked what he was researching, the subject said "the plot." He has not predicted any of it.

By Hollis Granger, Culture Desk · April 14, 2020 · 2 min read

Thirty-one days into isolation, the Daily Dirt Bag's Culture Desk has learned that Collin has now watched the same television series four times in full. When approached, he insisted this was not entertainment, but "research."

"I'm looking for details," Collin told our reporter over video chat, the same show playing quietly behind him. "There's a lot you miss the first time." Asked what he had learned on viewings two, three, and four, he said, "The plot."

The Culture Desk reached out to the show's fans online, where several confirmed that rewatching is a respected tradition. "You notice foreshadowing," wrote one. "You pick up on the music cues. You realize who was lying the whole time." Collin, asked whether he had noticed any of that, said, "The butler seemed off."

Culture Desk reporter Hollis Granger, who has watched the show once and enjoyed it, called the situation "admirable, in a way, and concerning in every other way."

A man can watch a thing four times, but he cannot call it research until he learns something. Collin has learned that the butler is not in it, and then forgotten.

Hollis Granger, Culture Desk

Friends have responded in different ways. Some have offered to watch with him "to help." Others have offered to hide the remote. One, who asked not to be named, said only: "I just want him to see the ending and feel something, and also to stop pausing it to tell me the butler theory."

Collin says he plans to continue his research into a fifth viewing, "with a notebook this time." He has not yet purchased a notebook. The Culture Desk will update this story as the plot develops.
