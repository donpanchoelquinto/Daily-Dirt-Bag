Collin Walks Into a Door, and the Door Wins

The door was open. Collin was not looking. The purple tape was a personal choice.

By Wendell Pike, Metro Desk · November 26, 2026 · 2 min read

Collin walked into a door on Wednesday evening. The door was open. Witnesses say it was a normal door, the kind with a handle and no particular ambition. Collin was walking with his phone in one hand and a conversation in the other, and the door, which had been there first, did not move out of the way.

"I saw it at the last second," Collin said from the living room couch, where he was recovering. Asked which second, he said, "the one after the door." The impact was reported by a family member as "a sound like somebody dropping a cabinet, but smaller."

The door was not hurt. The door is still standing, unbothered, and has already received an apology from no one.

Wendell Pike, Metro Desk

First aid, as administered by Collin

  • Gauze. One folded pad, applied with confidence and no mirror.
  • Purple tape. Wrapped around the head three times. Collin says the color was a practical decision. Asked what the practical benefit was, he said, "It is visible from the doorway."
  • Hydration. A tall blue cup and an orange bendy straw, held in both hands like a trophy.
  • Rest. Recommended by a doctor on the phone, who asked him to stop walking into things. Collin has agreed to stop walking into things at the next available opportunity.

Friends say the incident has changed Collin's approach to hallways. He now announces himself to every door before entering, which has made several doors visibly nervous. One neighbor reported that Collin said "excuse me" to the refrigerator on Thursday morning and then waited for it to respond.

The Daily Dirt Bag asked Collin whether he plans to file a complaint against the door. He said he would consider it, after he finishes the water, which he described as "very good water, somehow."

At press time the door had not commented. Collin was still on the couch, straw in place, watching the doorway with the focus of a man who has learned a lesson and would like the lesson to know it was his idea.
