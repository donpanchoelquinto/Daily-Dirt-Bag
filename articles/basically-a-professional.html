Collin Declares Himself "Basically a Professional" After Winning One Round of Mario Kart

The round was played on the easiest track, against a seven-year-old eating a sandwich.

By Wendell Pike, Sports & Games Desk · December 3, 2019 · 2 min read

In a stunning upset that nobody besides Collin will describe as stunning, the self-proclaimed "basically a professional" took first place in a single round of Mario Kart on Saturday night, defeating a field of two opponents: his cousin's seven-year-old, and a sandwich.

The race, held on the game's easiest track, concluded with Collin crossing the finish line in what he described as "a pretty comfortable lead." The seven-year-old, reached for comment, said he had been "distracted by lunch" and added, "I wasn't really playing."

Eyewitness accounts of the race vary only in their details. Everyone agrees that Collin took the lead early, that he celebrated at least twice before the finish line, and that at one point the seven-year-old paused to ask whether the game was "on." It was on. The seven-year-old had simply, in his words, "seen better."

Sports & Games Desk reporter Wendell Pike, who has covered video games for twenty years, was not present, but confirmed the result through a text message from the seven-year-old's mother, who wrote, "Oh yes, he won. He told everyone."

Collin's new credentials, as self-reported

  • Title: Basically a Professional
  • Record: 1 win, 0 losses, 0 other races attempted
  • Preferred shell: Green, "for the strategy"
  • Official sponsorship: None, but "open to offers"

He asked me to put 'Basically a Professional' on his birthday cake. I said I'd think about it. I have thought about it. The answer is no.

Wendell Pike, Sports & Games Desk

A rematch has been proposed, though terms remain in dispute. Collin has asked for "a harder track, to make it fair for everyone else." The seven-year-old has asked for the same easy track, a fresh sandwich, and, as a gesture of goodwill, a head start. Negotiations are ongoing.

Collin has formally requested that his new title be used in all future correspondence, including, he specified, "emails to my landlord." The Daily Dirt Bag has agreed to reference it in this article, once, and will not do so again.
