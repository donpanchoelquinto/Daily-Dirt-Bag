Collin's Army Is Four People, and It Is Going to Take Greenland

The force is himself and three friends. Greenland has not been told.

By Beatrice Okafor, Editor-in-Chief · November 26, 2026 · 2 min read

Collin is developing an army. It has four members, and one of them is Collin. The other three are friends who, according to the roster he keeps in his head, have already agreed by not arguing hard enough. With this force he intends to take over Greenland, a plan that currently exists as a feeling, a smile, and no map.

Editor-in-Chief Beatrice Okafor asked to see the order of battle. Collin said it was simple. "Me, and the boys." Pressed for ranks, he said everyone was a general, which means nobody has to salute, which means the army's first doctrine is convenience.

Four people can take a booth at a restaurant. Greenland is larger than a booth. He has not edited the plan for this.

Beatrice Okafor, Editor-in-Chief

The campaign, as currently imagined

  • Personnel. Collin, plus three friends. No one else has been invited, and no one else is needed, he says, which is the sentence of a man who has not checked the square mileage.
  • The objective. Greenland. He likes the shape. He has not called Greenland.
  • The date. "Soon," which the paper has learned to file next to "two minutes."
  • The dog. Present in the vehicle. Not on the roster. Collin says the dog is moral support and also not allowed to vote, a rule the dog has already ignored.

There is no route, no timetable, and no one in Nuuk waiting with a key. The Daily Dirt Bag asked what the army would do on arrival. Collin said they would "figure it out when we get there," which is also how he approaches parallel parking. His three friends, reached through him, because that is the only chain of command, are reportedly "down" in the way friends are down for a bit.

Okafor noted that an army of four, counting the commander twice if he is also the drummer, is less an invasion than a carpool. Collin accepted the note and promoted the carpool. Greenland remains a country. The husky remains unenlisted. The takeover remains a story he tells with his whole face, which is, so far, the only territory it has actually taken.
