Collin Takes Up Welding and Goes Straight Through the Subaru Brat

The sheet metal was supposed to stay metal. He gave a thumbs-up anyway.

By Dale Overholt, Investigations Editor · November 26, 2026 · 2 min read

Collin has taken up welding. The Subaru Brat has taken up ventilation. Investigators say the two developments are the same development, and that the sheet metal never had a chance.

The project, as explained beforehand, was modest. A patch. A little practice. "How hard can it be," Collin told a friend, which is a sentence this paper has learned to treat as a fire alarm. He put on the helmet, the respirator, and the gloves. He looked, by every account, like a man who had watched a video.

Then he welded through the panel. Not onto it. Through it. The Brat, a vehicle that has survived worse owners and worse decades, gained a hole where a bead was supposed to be. Collin lifted the helmet, looked at the new skylight in the sheet metal, and gave a thumbs-up.

He did not fix the hole. He posed with the hole. In this newsroom we call that a man who has accepted the work.

Dale Overholt, Investigations Editor

The official sequence

  • The plan. Practice on scrap. The scrap was, unfortunately, the car.
  • The first pass. Too hot, too long, and aimed at a panel that was already doing its job by being in one piece.
  • The sound. Described by a neighbor as "a sparkler having a bad idea."
  • The review. Thumbs-up. No notes. The Brat was not asked.

Asked whether he sucks at welding, Collin said he is "still in the learning phase," a phase the panel has already graduated from, into the phase of being a hole. He added that the respirator was "doing great," which nobody had disputed. The gear showed up. The skill is reportedly still on the truck.

A mechanic who asked not to be named, because he would like Collin to stop bringing him things, said the repair is now "a real repair, instead of a hobby." The Daily Dirt Bag will update this story if the Brat gets its metal back, or if Collin finds a second panel he trusts too much.
