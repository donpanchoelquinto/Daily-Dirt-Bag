A Study in Vanity: Collin Will Not Put the Phone Down

The mirror has been working overtime. He says he was only checking the lighting.

By Hollis Granger, Culture Desk · November 19, 2026 · 2 min read

The Daily Dirt Bag's Culture Desk can now confirm what the front-facing camera has known for years: Collin is in a committed relationship with his own reflection, and neither party is ready to see other people.

A photograph obtained this week shows him mid-session, phone raised, expression set to the particular seriousness of a man who has already deleted the last eleven tries. He told friends he was "just checking the lighting." The lighting, according to everyone else in the room, had been checked.

"He angles his head about four degrees, decides the universe has wronged him, and starts over," said Culture Desk reporter Hollis Granger. "I have watched shorter operas."

Vanity is a renewable resource. Collin is doing his part for the grid.

Hollis Granger, Culture Desk

What the camera roll suggests

  • Take one. Too serious. Deleted, then recovered, then deleted again so it would feel decisive.
  • Takes two through six. Same photo. He will argue they are different if you zoom in on an eyebrow.
  • Take seven. The one he posted. He asked three people if it looked "effortless." It did not.
  • Take eight. Taken after he said he was done. This is the one he actually likes.

Asked whether the uniform was the point, Collin said the uniform was "just what I had on," which is also what he says about the phone, the mirror, and the forty minutes. The Culture Desk is not questioning the clothes. The Culture Desk is questioning the encore.

At press time he had opened the front camera "one last time," a phrase witnesses say has no known ending.
