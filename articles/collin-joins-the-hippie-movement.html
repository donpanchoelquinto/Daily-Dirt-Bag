Collin Has Decided to Join the Hippie Movement

He announced it with both hands. The movement has not yet said whether it got the memo.

By Marguerite "Midge" Fontaine, Senior Dirt Correspondent · November 25, 2026 · 2 min read

Collin has decided to join the hippie movement, effective whenever he finishes explaining it. He made the announcement the way he makes most announcements: with a photo, a tie he did not loosen, and complete confidence that the rest of the plan would occur to him later.

Witnesses say the conversion took one evening. There was no drum circle. There was a patio, a string of lights, and Collin informing the group that he had "been thinking about peace." Asked what he had been thinking, he held up both hands and made the sign, which he then held until someone took the picture.

He joined a movement the way other people join a group chat. He showed up, made a gesture, and waited to be thanked.

The platform, as currently understood

  • Peace. To be expressed with the hands, ideally while seated, ideally more than once.
  • The tie. Staying. He says a movement should have standards.
  • The phone. Also staying. Enlightenment, he argues, still needs a good angle.
  • Further demands. To be announced after he looks up what the original movement actually wanted.

Friends report that he has already used the phrase "good vibes only" one time, immediately after someone asked him to help move a chair. The chair was not moved. The vibes, he said, were not ready.

The hippie movement itself could not be reached for comment, mostly because Collin has not yet told it he joined. He said he would send a note "when the aesthetic is locked." The Culture Desk will update this story if a second belief arrives.
